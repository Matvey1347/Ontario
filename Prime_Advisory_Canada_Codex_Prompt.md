### D. What to Do After a Scam
**H2:** What to Do If You've Been Scammed Online

If you've been scammed out of money, act promptly. Taking the right steps early may help protect your accounts and preserve important evidence.

1. Contact your bank or payment provider.
2. Secure your financial and online accounts.
3. Preserve transaction records and communications.
4. Report suspected fraud to the appropriate official authorities.
5. Consider a professional case assessment to understand additional options.

Clarify that contacting Prime Advisory Canada is not the same as filing an official police or government report.
